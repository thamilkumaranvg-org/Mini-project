How It Works

Book once, arrive on time, no waiting in line.

Step 1 of 5

Register & Log in

Create your farmer profile once with your name, mobile, email and address, then log in.

Step 2 of 5

Choose Day & Session

Pick a day this week and a session (09:00-11:00, 11:20-13:20 or 14:00-16:00). Each session shows Available, Almost full or Full. Only 8 farmers per session.

Step 3 of 5

Enter Crop & Quantity

Select your crop and the quantity in kg. See the estimated price in rupees (MSP rate) before you confirm the booking.

Step 4 of 5

Get Your Receipt & QR

Your receipt with a QR code is ready instantly. It is valid only for your booked session. If you miss the session, book a new slot.

Step 5 of 5

Arrive, Scan & Get Paid

Come to the office during your session and show the QR at the gate. A green tick confirms you, and you get your queue number. When the officer calls you, your produce is weighed and you are paid in cash. You are notified at every step.
